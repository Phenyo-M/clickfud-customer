You may cancel an eligible order from within the app. Currently, an order can be cancelled at any point while it is still marked “Received” or “Preparing” — that is, before the shop has marked it ready for collection.   • Cash orders: cancelling is free, since nothing has been charged yet.
  • Card orders: cancelling a paid order incurs a cancellation fee (currently 20% of the order total, which may change), and the remaining amount is refunded to your original payment method via Paystack. Refunds are queued immediately but confirmed by Paystack and may take several days to reflect.

Note: clickFud intends to move to a policy where cancellation is only free within the first two minutes of placing an order. That specific two-minute window is not yet enforced by the app — the rule above (cancellable any time up to “ready,” with a fee on paid card orders) is what the app currently does.

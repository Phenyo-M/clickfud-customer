## 6. Payments

You may pay by **cash** (paid in person at collection) or by **card**, processed by our payment processor, Paystack. Card payments are charged at the time of checkout; your order is only created once payment is confirmed. We do not receive or store your full card details. If Paystack indicates your card is eligible to be saved, we may store limited, non-sensitive card details (last 4 digits, bank, card type, expiry) so you can reuse it later — you can remove a saved card at any time from your Profile.

If a card payment fails, no order is created and no charge is made.

## 7. Cancellations

You may cancel an eligible order from within the app. Currently, an order can be cancelled at any point while it is still marked "Received" or "Preparing" — that is, before the shop has marked it ready for collection. Once an order has been marked ready, it can no longer be cancelled through the app.

- **Cash orders:** cancelling is free, since nothing has been charged yet.
- **Card orders:** cancelling a paid order incurs a cancellation fee (currently 20% of the order total, which may change), and the remaining amount is refunded to your original payment method via Paystack. Refunds are queued immediately but confirmed by Paystack and may take several days to reflect.

**Note:** clickFud intends to move to a policy where cancellation is only free within the first two minutes of placing an order. That specific two-minute window is **not yet enforced by the app** — the rule above (cancellable any time up to "ready," with a fee on paid card orders) is what the app currently does. If you have questions about your specific order, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

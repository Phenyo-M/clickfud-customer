What ClickFood does

ClickFood lets you browse shops on and around university campuses, order food and collect it. ClickFood runs the app. The shops prepare and sell the food. Student sellers are students who sell food through ClickFood and are paid by buyers directly.

Your account

  • Use an email address you can open. You may need to verify it.
  • Give true information and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One person, one account. Don't use someone else's account.
  • ClickFood is meant for people aged 18 and older. If you are under 18, use it only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Orders

  • The price at checkout is what you pay, including any platform fee shown there.
  • Your order is sent to the shop once it is placed. For card payments, the order is placed only after the payment is confirmed.
  • The shop may be busy, closed or out of an item. If a shop can't fulfil your order, it will be cancelled.
  • All orders are for collection. Bring your collection code. Waiting times are estimates.

The shop's responsibilities

Each shop is responsible for its menu, prices, food, allergens information, food safety and for preparing your order correctly. ClickFood checks shops before they go live, but ClickFood does not make the food.

Collecting your order

  • When your order is ready, you have a set time to collect it (currently 30 minutes).
  • If you miss it, the app asks if you still want to collect it. You can choose a new time (currently up to 2 times), or tell us you won't collect it.
  • If you don't answer within the time shown, the order is closed as not collected.
  • Food that was ready but not collected is not refunded.

Payments

  • Card payments are processed by PayFast on its own secure page. ClickFood never sees your card details.
  • You can also choose Cash on Collection and pay the shop when you collect.
  • Student sellers are paid by you directly (for example in cash or by transfer). ClickFood does not take or hold that payment.
  • Group orders are paid by card. Either the group leader pays for everyone or each person pays their own share, as chosen for that group.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel in the app while your order is "Received" or "Preparing". Once it is ready, it can't be cancelled.
  • Cash orders: cancelling is free.
  • Card orders: a cancellation fee applies (currently 20% of the order). The rest is refunded to your card. Refunds can take a few working days.
  • If something is wrong with your order, such as a missing or wrong item, ask for a refund from the order in the app. The shop reviews it. If you are not happy with the answer, contact us.
  • These terms do not take away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, for example if food is unsafe or not what was described.

Promotions and student offers

  • Each offer has its own rules, which can include dates, products, a minimum spend, how many times you can use it and how many are available in total.
  • Some offers are for verified students only. To use them, verify your student status with your university email. Choosing a university in your profile is not enough.
  • Offers may end when they run out or reach their end date.
  • If an offer is used against its rules, for example with fake or shared accounts, we may cancel the discount, the order or the account.

Student Entrepreneur Programme

Who can apply

Students with a ClickFood student account at a participating university or college (currently the University of Pretoria, Tshwane University of Technology, Rosebank College, Eduvos and the University of South Africa) who verify their official student email address. Personal email addresses cannot be used to apply.

Applying

  • You apply in the app. ClickFood reviews each application and may approve or reject it. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval.
  • Your information must be true and complete. We may withdraw an approval or close a shop if it is not.
  • You can have one open application and one student shop at a time. You can withdraw your application before a decision.

Before you can sell

  • Only approved applicants can set up a student shop. ClickFood checks the shop, its photos and products before it goes live.
  • You must follow the seller rules shown in the app: sell only food and non-alcoholic drinks you prepare yourself, describe food honestly, list allergens and never sell alcohol, tobacco, medicine or anything illegal.
  • You are responsible for preparing, storing and handing over food safely and for meeting any food safety and other legal requirements that apply to you. Telling us about your food preparation in your application does not mean ClickFood has checked or approved that you meet these requirements.
  • Buyers pay you directly. ClickFood does not take or hold payment for student food.

Suspending or closing a shop

We may hide, suspend or close a student shop, or withdraw your place in the programme, if you break these terms or the seller rules, if food is unsafe, if there are serious or repeated complaints, if your information was false, or if you are no longer a student. Unless it would be unsafe or unlawful, we will tell you why, and you can ask us to look at it again.

Things you must not do

  • Give false information, or pretend to be someone else.
  • Use another person's account, payment card or student email.
  • Misuse offers, refunds or cancellations.
  • Harass shop staff, sellers or other users, or post abusive or false reviews.
  • Try to break into, overload or interfere with ClickFood.

Suspending accounts

If you break these terms, misuse the app or put others at risk, we may limit, suspend or close your account. Unless it would be unsafe or unlawful, we will tell you why, and you can contact us to ask us to look at it again.

Reviews

Reviews must be honest and about your own order. We may remove reviews that are abusive, false or share someone's personal information.

Locations and directions

Distances in the app are straight-line estimates. Directions open in Google Maps. Always check that a route is safe.

We work to keep ClickFood running, but it may sometimes be unavailable or have errors. Features can change over time.

Responsibility

ClickFood is responsible for running the app. Shops and student sellers are responsible for their food and for fulfilling their orders. Nothing in these terms limits any responsibility that the law does not allow us to limit.

Help and complaints

For help with an order, use the order in the app first. For anything else, or if you are not happy with an outcome, email support@clickfood.co.za. Include your order number if your question is about an order.

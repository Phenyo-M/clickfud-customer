Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 (Student Entrepreneur Programme added)

This policy explains what personal information ClickFood collects when you use the app, why we need it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It describes how ClickFood works today.

Who we are

ClickFood is a food ordering app for collecting food from shops on and around university campuses in South Africa. ClickFood is the responsible party for the personal information described here.

  • Business name: ClickFood
  • Information officer: Mokoena Phenyo
  • Privacy requests and help: support@clickfood.co.za
  • General questions: info@clickfood.co.za

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in a protected, scrambled form.
  • Your phone number and profile photo, if you add them.
  • If you sign in with Google, the name, email address and photo Google shares with us.

Student details

  • Whether you are a student, and the university and campus you choose.
  • If you verify your student status: the university email address you enter, when it was verified, and whether it is still valid. We email a one-time link to that address. We keep only a scrambled copy of the link's code, never the link itself.
  • If your login email is a university student-number email, the student number taken from it.

Your address and location

  • The address and map pin you save as your home location. We use it to show the shops closest to you.
  • Your phone's current location, only if you tap a button such as "Use my location" or "Directions" and allow it. It stays in the app's memory while you use it and is not saved on our systems.

Orders and payments

  • What you order, from which shop, the price, any promo code used, your chosen collection time, the collection code and the order's progress.
  • For card payments: the payment reference, amount and status from our payment provider. We never see or store your card number, CVV or PIN.
  • Refund requests, cancellations and the reason you give.
  • Group orders: your share of the order and whether it has been paid.
  • Promotion claims: which offers you used and on which order, so each offer's limits can be applied.

Things you add

  • Ratings and reviews.
  • Your class timetable, if you use My Timetable, and timetables you choose to share by link.
  • If you sell food as a student: your shop details, photos, products, WhatsApp number and pickup point.
  • If you delete your account, the reason you choose (stored without your name).

Technical and security information

  • Sign-in attempts. Your email address and network address are stored only as scrambled codes, to slow down people guessing passwords.
  • A security check (CAPTCHA) when you sign up or sign in, to stop automated sign-ups.
  • Error reports when something breaks in the app. These leave out email addresses, phone numbers, passwords and sign-in tokens.
  • Notification details from your browser if you turn on phone alerts.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show shops and menus, with the most relevant shops first.
  • To send your order to the shop, take payment, handle cancellations and refunds, and help you collect your food.
  • To check that student offers go to verified students and that each offer's limits are respected.
  • To send you order updates in the app, by email or as phone alerts, depending on your notification settings.
  • To protect accounts, find suspicious activity and stop misuse of the app and its offers.
  • To fix problems and keep the app working.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Student Entrepreneur Programme applications

If you apply to sell food as a Student Entrepreneur, we use your application to decide whether you can join the programme and, if you are approved, to help set up your shop.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and university.
  • Your verified university student email address. We confirm it by emailing a one-time link to that address, which you must open while signed in. We do not contact your university.
  • Your year of study, how long you expect to remain at university and how many years you have left. These are your own estimates. We use them to understand how long you are likely to run your shop while you are a student.
  • Your food business name, the food you plan to sell, your business description, why you think it suits students, how you will prepare and store food safely, and the days and times you expect to sell.
  • That you confirmed your information, accepted the Terms and read this policy, with the version and time.
  • Our review: the decision, the reviewer and any note. A note is shown to you only if the reviewer chooses to share it.

Population group (optional)

The application asks for your population group. You do not have to answer, and it is stored only if you separately agree. We use it only to understand which students the programme reaches. It is never used to approve or reject an application. Only ClickFood reviewers can see it, it is kept separately from the rest of your application, and it is deleted automatically 90 days after a decision or after you withdraw. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Who sees your application

Only you and ClickFood's reviewers. Shops and other customers cannot see it. If you are approved and your shop goes live, the shop details you choose to publish (such as your shop name, description, photos and products) become visible to customers.

How long we keep it

Applications are kept while your account is open, so we have a record of each decision. They are deleted when you delete your account.

Who can see your information

Shops you order from

The shop sees your name, phone number, what you ordered, your collection time and your collection code, so it can prepare and hand over your order. A student seller you contact on WhatsApp also sees your WhatsApp number. When you tap to contact a student seller, you get their WhatsApp number.

Your group

People in a group order with you can see your name and your share of that order.

Service providers

We use other companies to run parts of ClickFood. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Hosting and database: stores the app's data, accounts and files.
  • Payments: PayFast processes card payments and refunds on its own secure page.
  • Email delivery: sends sign-up, password, verification and order emails.
  • Security check: runs the CAPTCHA check on sign-up and sign-in.
  • Maps: map images come from OpenStreetMap. When you search for an address on a map, the words you type are sent to OpenStreetMap's search service. Directions open in Google Maps, which receives the destination and, if you allowed it, your current location.
  • Google sign-in: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Phone alerts: your browser's notification service delivers alerts.
  • AI assistance for technical problems: when the app reports a technical problem, an AI service may summarise the technical details for our team. Names, email addresses, phone numbers and payment details are removed first.
  • RecessBox: our separate storage booking service receives your verified email address only if you choose to open it from ClickFood.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect people from harm or fraud.

Information stored outside South Africa

ClickFood's database and email services are located in the European Union (Ireland). Some service providers may process information in other countries. We use providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that protect your information.

Location

You never have to share your phone's location to use ClickFood. If you don't allow it, you can still browse by university and campus, and distances use the home address you saved. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone or browser settings.

How we protect your information

We limit who can see what: you see your own account and orders, a shop sees only the orders placed with it, and our team's access is restricted by role. Connections to ClickFood are encrypted. We use sign-in limits, security checks and monitoring to protect accounts.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security problem affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.

Storage on your device

The app saves a few things on your own phone or computer so it works smoothly: your sign-in, cart, theme, favourites and a copy of menus for offline browsing. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information stays while your account is open.
  • Order and payment records are kept after an account is deleted, without your name and contact details, because we need them for accounting, refunds and disputes.
  • Security and sign-in records are deleted automatically after a short period.
  • Student verification links stop working after 24 hours.

Your rights

  • See and correct: you can view and change your name, phone, photo, student status, university, campus and address in Profile.
  • Delete: you can delete your account in Profile. This removes your login and personal details. Order records are kept without your name, as explained above.
  • Notifications: you can switch in-app, email and phone alerts on or off in Profile.
  • Ask us: you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing support@clickfood.co.za.
  • Complain: if you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za.

Children

ClickFood is meant for people aged 18 and older. If you are under 18, please use ClickFood only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will deal with it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app before they apply and show the new date at the top of this page.
